# Design Principles

## Approval-first external actions

Public posts, outbound messaging, and irreversible actions should require explicit confirmation.

## Automate 80% of work, verify final 20%

Let automation handle discovery, draft prep, formatting, and routing.

## Keep updates concise and actionable

Every notification should answer:
1) What changed?
2) Why it matters?
3) What next action is recommended?

## Favor repeatable systems over ad-hoc tasks

Use scheduled jobs, templates, and reusable skill logic.
